# Cohort 005 Project

This platform sells online courses to learners. The domain covers purchasing, access, and the money-back process.

## Language

**Refund**:
A learner's request to get their money back for a course purchase. It is filed manually via the course page, reviewed by a human, and may be approved or declined. Access to the course is retained until the request is resolved.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation
